Doc 5 · could you start today?

Start today.

Sebastian asked whether you could put your name on a curl cream and start selling right now. Short answer: almost. You could order today and have branded stock in about two to three weeks.

Download PDF

Mock-up: version 1 in a supplier's standard bottle with a cream label.

The honest answer

Nobody will dropship a curl cream with your label to Aussie customers at a price that makes sense. The no-minimum dropship platforms (Blanka, Supliful and others) either don't sell a curl cream or ship from the US at about US$26 an order. That's around $71 per unit before you've made a sale.

The real option is 1.5 hours up the road. Private Label Dynamics at Coolum Beach, on the Sunshine Coast, makes a stock Leave-in Curl Cream (250ml, with shea, argan, rice protein and Kakadu plum). It'll put your label on it, and you can order 10 at a time or a single branded sample. It doesn't dropship, so you post the orders yourself. At the start that's a good thing, because every parcel can include a handwritten note.

Steps

  1. Get an ABN (abr.gov.au, free, often issued the same day). The supplier only sells to businesses.
  2. Contact PLD. Ask for: prices in AUD or USD, whether GST is included, whether a cream/brown label fits their template, the full ingredient list, and whether you can pick up from Coolum.
  3. Order one branded sample (about $10 plus $50 for branding), or go straight to 10 units.
  4. Design the label in Canva using the aesthetic doc: the wordmark and CURL CREAM on the front, the three steps and the ingredients on the back.
  5. Set up Shopify (free trial, then about $56/mo) with a pre-order and a clear ship date.
  6. Post the TikTok pack with "link in bio" while the labels print (7–10 business days).
  7. Ship with Australia Post (about $11.70 for a parcel up to 500g).

Day-one cost: about $100–250. Time to stock in hand: 2–3 weeks.

Version 1 → version 2

Version 1: the stock formula in the supplier's bottle or tube with your label. It's fast and cheap, and it proves people want it. Version 2: the cream-and-brown jar and a custom scent and texture from a Gold Coast lab (Cosmetic Alchemist has no minimum order, with about 4–8 weeks of development). By then you'll know from real customers what to change.
