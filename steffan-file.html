Eight things we noticed

1. You're already the customer.

From your curly-hair storytime (Nov 2025): "I have to shower every morning to sort of get my hair to set, and then I just let it dry naturally." That's the Wave Set routine word for word, and it's where the name earns its keep: you set it.

2. Hair content already works for you.

"The cons of having curly hair" (the windy before-and-after) did 276k views and 52k likes. That's about a 19% like rate, which is very high for any account. At least a dozen of your videos are about your hair, and they keep coming back to three villains: wind, humidity and the brush.

3. You have a catchphrase and you don't know it.

"I look like a toilet brush." "Animals be nesting in there." "This piece of hair is just there like, here I am." "Me when someone says I have a perm." "Why is my hair kinda giving the Riddler." No brand copywriter could write lines like these. We used every one of them.

4. Your biggest hits are formats, not luck.

The wedding lineup, the Rio characters and the Katy Perry costumes all went huge because they're repeatable formats you ran as series ("i swear this is the last one"). Wave Set should do the same: a curl lineup, reasons to get curls, a storytime series.

5. The look is already on-brand.

White singlets, faded tees, a moustache since late 2025, a cap at golden hour, sun-bleached curls. It's the Rhythm and Banks palette without trying. Your feed already has the blues: the Point Lookout-style water, the pool, the sky behind you at the beach.

6. You're local, and it shows.

Miami (too windy that day), the Brisbane River, Cabarita, the Wendy's run, the drive-thru that took forever. SEQ is in the content already, which makes a Gold Coast and Redlands story believable.

7. You commit to the bit.

Wigs, costumes, a Morphsuit rating, skits built around a Jet2 advert sound. A brand run by someone who'll dress up as every Rio bird can do launch stunts nobody else in haircare would try.

8. You turned 21 in July.

"It's my birth month #brisbane #21." So this is a founder who's 21 with tens of millions of views behind him. That's the story the press would pick up.

What we took from it

The founder is the brand. The closest competitor, Wavy in the UK, was co-founded by a guy famous for his curls, and its website still hides him. Every men's curl brand site is either a lab or a barbershop. Nobody in the category is funny. You are.

So everything in this pack runs on one line from your own routine: Don't brush it. Set it.
