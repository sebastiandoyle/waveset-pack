The posting plan.

Six TikToks cut from your own videos, nine Instagram grid posts, two carousels and three story templates, plus when to post each one and what to say.

Download PDF

Rules for 2026

  • Post from your own account first, as Instagram Collab posts with @waveset. A new brand account can't use Trial Reels until it has 1,000 followers, and your account already has the reach.
  • Keywords in the caption beat hashtags. Write the words people search for ("men's curly hair", "curl cream", "surfer hair", "gold coast") into the caption. Instagram now caps hashtags at 5 anyway.
  • Upload Instagram posts at 4:5. The profile grid now crops to 3:4, so keep text away from the edges. All the posts here are built that way.
  • Add the trending sound in the app so it counts as trending audio. Our files use your original audio or a royalty-free bed.
  • TikTok Shop isn't live in Australia (TikTok ANZ, Sep 2026). Send people to a link in bio.

Sources: SocialPilot trends Sep 2026 · Z Media on TikTok Shop AU
