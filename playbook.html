Surf shops that fit the look

These longboard and single-fin shops suit the brand best: Mt Woodgee (Burleigh and Currumbin), Duke's Longboards and Miami Longboards (Miami and Mermaid), Bob Minty Surfboards (Point Lookout, Straddie), Cylinder Surf (Cleveland), Coolangatta Surf. Walk in with a jar and a phone showing your views.

Barbers to seed

Brisbane: Togninis, Elysium Hair (curl-by-curl cutting), Twin Palms (James St), Crown and Co. Gold Coast: Busy Barber, Ace of Fades (Burleigh), Legend Cutz (Mermaid). None are confirmed men's-curl specialists, so give them samples and see who gets it.

The name question

"Wave set" is a dictionary word for a hair-setting lotion (Merriam-Webster), so as a plain word mark for hair products it's likely to be knocked back as descriptive. IP Australia shows 0 results for "waveset" and one lapsed "SET WAVE AUSTRALIA" in clothing. The name isn't taken, it's just hard to own.

The move: use a distinctive house name with Wave Set as the product. These returned zero results on IP Australia's quick search:

  • Glassoff: "Glassoff Wave Set curl cream" (glass-off is the glassy evening water)
  • Swellset
  • Lefthander (the only existing mark is lapsed, and in wine)
  • Or your surname: Doyle's Wave Set

Do a TM Headstart first ($200 + $130 per class). It's a cheap pre-check before a full application. Domains: waveset.com has been held by a corporate registrar since 2000, but getwaveset.com and wavesetco.com looked free, and waveset.com.au doesn't resolve.

Sources: Merriam-Webster · IP Australia search · IP Australia fees

The rules (short version)

  • Buy from an Australian maker and relabel it, and the maker handles AICIS registration. If you import, you register yourself (about $85/yr).
  • Your label must list every ingredient (ACCC standard): 1% and over in descending order, then the rest, with colours last. Your online listing needs the list too.
  • Appearance claims only: "defines", "less frizz", "holds". Never treats, repairs, protects or SPF.
  • "Made in Australia" works if the maker formulates and fills here. The green kangaroo logo is a paid licence ($330/yr).

Sources: AICIS · ACCC cosmetics labelling · TGA
