20 questions for Steffan.

Your answers decide what we build next. Voice-note your answers to Sebastian if that's easier.

Download PDF

You and the brand

  1. Are you the face of Wave Set? Everything here assumes yes: your hair, your humour, your account.
  2. How much of your personal TikTok are you happy to point at the brand? One post in five? Every hair video?
  3. Wave Set as the brand name, or as the product under a house name like Glassoff or Doyle's?
  4. Is anyone else in it with you? Mates, a partner, Sebastian?
  5. Three words you want people to feel when they open it?

The product

  1. Jar or bottle for version 1? The fastest supplier only does bottles.
  2. What should it smell like? Coconut, salt, nothing, something else?
  3. Soft and moving, or more hold? What does Frizz Ease get wrong for you?
  4. Is it only for curls like yours, or for wavy hair too?
  5. One product, or a family later (sea salt spray, a travel size)?

The look

  1. Did we read the colours right: blue feed, cream-and-brown product?
  2. Which of the three websites feels most like you, and what would you steal from the others?
  3. Would you shoot new content at Straddie or Burleigh for launch, or keep it all from your existing videos?

Money and timing

  1. What's your budget: roughly $3k, $10k or $25k?
  2. When do you want the first jar sold? The first day of summer (1 Dec) is a good target.
  3. Price: $34, $38, or something else?

Selling it

  1. Markets, surf shops, barbers, online only, or all of them?
  2. Do you want a stall at The Coastal Markets Burleigh on 25 Oct or 29 Nov?
  3. Are there any surf shops or barbers you already know?
  4. What would make this a win for you in six months?
